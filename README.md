**🔤 Jargon Jumble**

A terminal-based word-scramble game powered by an LLM. Instead of relying on a fixed word list, the game asks an AI model to generate a new word and hint from any category the player chooses: fruits, animals, countries, programming terms, or anything else.

**How it works**
Pick a category and a difficulty (easy, medium, hard).
Each game has 5 rounds. A scrambled word is shown and you have 3 attempts to solve it.
After a wrong guess, you get a clue: the word’s first letter and length.
Type hint for a hint (costs points), skip to move on, or quit to leave.
Faster solves earn more points, and consecutive correct answers earn a streak bonus.
At the end, the AI comments on your results in software-testing language, and you can play again and try to beat your best score.


**Features**

AI-generated words and hints for unlimited categories
Three difficulty levels
Multi-attempt rounds with progressive clues
Scoring system with streak bonuses and a session high score
Input validation and retry logic for unreliable AI responses
